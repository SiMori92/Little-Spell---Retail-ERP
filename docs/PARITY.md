# Behavior parity contract — M4/M5 checkpoint

Updated 2026-11-17 (Asia/Hong_Kong). Source catalogue: frozen v1.9.4, including CR-A and Addendum J. J is approved in principle but not built in the reference; D.1 stays operative until the first-real-statement decision and composition evidence. Do not silently implement J.

This is a complete source inventory, not a claim of functional parity. DEFERRED rows are founder-reviewable milestone allocations, not waivers. Django migrations are dropped as technology, never as business rules. Test modules remain explicit replacement-test obligations. A foundation journal schema does not establish posting/report parity.

M1 closes supplier/product/channel/account masters, supplier/product CSV intake and command replacements, the current two master header contracts, and chart materialization. Whole modules that also contain unbuilt business behavior remain DEFERRED, even when their master-data subset is built. Purchasing rows previously misallocated to M1/M2 are explicitly assigned to M3 below.

Setup includes persisted business details and preparation. M3 adds a separate complete, evidenced and explicitly confirmed physical count that books opening inventory; M1 preparation still never books anything by itself. A no-stock declaration only completes setup declarations, not financial readiness. The channels/accounts CSV contracts are v2-authored additions, not pre-existing reference contracts.

M3 carries the approved supplier-deposit account from the reference migration as an append-only chart addendum. The original 74-row snapshot/bootstrap is unchanged; the approved current chart becomes 75 rows when first adopted. Operational WAC includes M2 dispatch deductions once and labels their COGS unposted; M4 now posts COGS once; complete financial report parity remains a final gate. Zero-only packaging counts, no carrier/duty/in-transit matching, and one receipt/invoice per PO line are explicit scope boundaries.


M4/M5 adds money facts and receipts, immutable public FX, separate posting, exact operational COGS integration, accounting reversals, computed close diagnostics/attempts, dataset transition, Today, 18 report screens and dashboard charts. Historical run bytes remain unchanged; reviewed inventory attribution is appended separately. G3 purchasing fixtures independently tie to the reference at four decimal places. Full Etsy/all-fixture parity remains BLOCKED by the frozen importer/posting mismatch and missing real settlement composition. A BUILT screen or rule does not assert that the final RB gate passed. Whole modules stay DEFERRED where their complete reference protocol is not reproduced.
